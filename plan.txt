FeatureOS — Complete Development Roadmap (Engineering Project Plan v1.0)

Project: FeatureOS — AI-Native Feature Management & Autonomous Rollout Platform

Objective: Build a production-grade, AI-powered feature management platform that autonomously analyzes telemetry, discovers affected user cohorts, plans progressive rollouts, performs self-healing rollbacks, and provides enterprise-grade observability.

Target Audience: Recruiters and interviewers for Backend, Platform Engineering, AI Engineering, and Distributed Systems roles.
